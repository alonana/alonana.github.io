Today I used 2.3% methylene blue to color the sample.
First I diluted it with 1 part color and 26 parts water, but it did not color much.
I tried again using 3 parts color and 26 parts water, and the results were a bit better.

I could not find many moving cells. Maybe my sample jar got too old and I need to start a new one?

The color seems to stick mostly to the cells, and it did color some inner parts.
