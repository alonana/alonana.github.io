# Sweet Greenhouse, checking again 🔬

Today I checked the [sugar trio]({% post_url 2026-11-06-sugar %}) again.
I added some methylene blue, like in the [color post]({% post_url 2026-11-16-color %}).

I started with zero sugar, then moved to the second tube and finally the one with the most sugar.
The difference was clear: the more sugar, the less life activity I could see.

No worms in any of them. That was a bit of a surprise.

I also saw these huge cells. Maybe protozoa? I am not sure which kind.
