# Blood

My wife but her finger, so she decided to give a sample.
The problem is that I've checked it only about 10 hours later, and it is all dry.

All over I could see what looks like a mass of rounded items connected as a big mass with multiple layers.
My guess is that this is the work of blood palettes.

![sample]({{ '/assets/images/2026-11-04/b1.png' | relative_url }})
![sample]({{ '/assets/images/2026-11-04/b2.png' | relative_url }})

On the very edge of the glass sample, I could locate a more individually distinguishable cells.
I can even see the darker center, and I am pretty sure these are red blood cells.
I wonder why are they only on the edges while the entire sample show only the first type?

![sample]({{ '/assets/images/2026-11-04/b3.png' | relative_url }})


# Counting Worms

I want to focus on worms for now.
The first thing I want to understand is worms count and movement.
I will collect a sample from my jar after a gentle mix, and check status.
I plan to repeat this multiple times and compare results.
I might create new jars samples and compare them overtime.


Change of plan:

Apparently, the gentle mix had raise so many worms, that it is very hard to count.
I will use a different approach: I will scan squares using X40 magnification and will use average.
The scanned square size is 1mm X 1mm.
I will scan from the edge, and move 1 square each time.
I will report only squares with worms of length at least 100 micrometer.
