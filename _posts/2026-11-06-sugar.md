# Sweet Greenhouse

Another 3 test tubes, this time checking for the affects of sugar on worms.
I created 3 tubes, each with similar amount of soil and 4ml water.

I don't have a way to measure small amount of sugar, so I've mixed 2g water and 2g simple white sugar.
And updated as follows:
1. 0 sugar added
2. 0.25 ml mix added, hence expected 0.12 ml sugar 
3. 0.5 ml mix added, hence expected 0.25 ml sugar

I will track in about one week, and check results.
I have a feeling that the sugar will not do well for the worms, but I am not sure.
We'll have to wait and see.
