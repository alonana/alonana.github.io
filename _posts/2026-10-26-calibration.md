## Calibration games


Today I've calibrated the Swift software to estimate the real items size.
To do this I've used the supplied glass slide with measurement marks.
The entire length is 1mm, so I can use this to understand items real size.

First, on the left menu, select Capture and Resolution.
Under the "Live" combo, select the max resolution.
Then click on the calibrate icon on the top, and mark the scale to specify the length.

![calibrate image]({{ '/assets/images/2026-10-26/calibrate.png' | relative_url }})


To update a calibration, first delete the existing one.
Click on settings, magnification, and delete, or clear all.

![calibrate image]({{ '/assets/images/2026-10-26/settings.png' | relative_url }})

I repeated this for all magnifications to calibrate the actual size for each.
