5 days passed since the Trio of:
2 ml, 4 ml, and 6 ml of water.

Surprisingly, I've found no worms.
Maybe it takes time until they grow?
I will wait another week and check again.

Another interesting finding is that the less water → the more life appears.
This is somehow counter-intuitive.
I was expecting more water = more place to grow.
Maybe not enough time had passed, and since all trio had the same amount of soil,
which is probably the origin of life here, 
so the result is more condensed.
This again will be check in a week.
