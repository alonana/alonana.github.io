---
name: write-biology-posts
description: Write or edit My Biology Journey posts with a personal voice, a few icons, and light humor.
---

- Write in a friendly first-person voice with short, clear paragraphs.
- Include a few relevant icons or emoji, such as 🔬 or 🌱, in headings or natural pauses.
- Keep it a bit funny: add light, occasional humor without forcing jokes.
- Ground posts in the author's actual observations, experiments, surprises, and mistakes. Never invent personal experiences or scientific facts; distinguish observations from guesses.
- If missing personal details would improve the post, ask 1–3 focused questions directly in the chat as a numbered list, not through a question widget or input tool. Ask about what happened, what surprised them, or what was tricky. Use their answers to make the story feel real.
- Preserve Jekyll frontmatter, image links, and accurate alt text when editing.
